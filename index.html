Key

Tier (the play score, NEW & UNPROVEN)
A sorting aid, not a bet rule, registered after Week 6 (DL-Y-019). Tier A = the team is an underdog of 7+ and our side is the under (the one angle that has held up). PASS = underdog of 7+ and our side is the over. B = everything else. The tier is fixed at the first pull where the gap reaches 10 and graded against that line. It needs 200 graded lines to prove out; until then treat it as a guess. Gap size is TRACKED, not scored — big disagreements are not rewarded or penalised.
Gap
Our number minus FanDuel's line. Negative: FanDuel is higher, so our side is the under. Positive: the over.
Ours
The middle outcome from the matchup: expected carries times expected yards per carry, adjusted for opponents faced, then corrected for spread and total. Sacks count against rushing.
Raw · Raw + spread
Raw is the team's rushing yards per game averaged with what the opponent allows. Raw + spread corrects that for the spread and total.
Game script
Read from the spread, same rule as the matchup dashboard. A favorite runs more late, which pushes rushing up; an underdog abandons the run, which pushes it down. Under 7 points is a close game with no lean; 14 or more shows a double arrow. "With us" means the script pushes the same way as our side of the line. "Rain leans run" shows at a 50%+ rain chance. The spread is already inside our number, so this is context for the bet and not extra yards.
Middle half
The band where the result lands half the time. A line inside it is within one game's normal swing of our number.
Book beyond all 3
FanDuel's line is 20 or more yards above, or 20 or more below, every one of our three numbers: ours, raw, and raw + spread.
Weather
Wind in mph, gusts where the site lists them, and chance of rain. Highlighted at 15+ mph wind, 25+ mph gusts or 50%+ rain. Not in our number.
Mobile QB · QB change
Tracked, not in the number or the tier (DL-Y-022). The expected starter is the QB who started the team's most recent FBS game (most dropbacks). MOBILE QB: he averages 35+ rushing yards or 6+ carries per FBS start this season, counting designed runs and scrambles only (no sacks, no kneel-downs), with at least 2 starts; with fewer starts the row shows "QB: 1 start" instead. QB CHANGE: the expected starter is not the QB with the most starts this season. "QB unconfirmed" links a dated report that the starter is questionable or not named. Highlighted on under rows, where a running QB adds rushing yards the box score counts.
Run-first
The team ran on 65% or more of its plays this season (rush attempts including sacks, divided by rush plus pass attempts, FBS games before this week), or it is an option team: Army, Navy, Air Force or Rice. Our number has priced these teams badly, so the Short list hides them unless you press "Show run-first teams". Tracked, not in the number or the tier.
Shadow C5 · C3 (shadow – not the bet number)
Two candidate numbers shown in small type under ours, never used for the tier or the bet (DL-Y-025). C5 estimates carries from the game script (spread, total and how often the team runs) instead of from season carries. C3 is our formula fit on the last three seasons only. C5 replaces ours only if, after 300+ graded lines from Week 9, it wins 2+ points more often at 10+ gaps and misses by no more than half a yard more.
Stubborn runner · Quick to abandon
How often the team still runs when it trails by 8 or more this season (FBS games, all four quarters, sacks counted as passes, 40+ trailing plays needed). Stubborn runner: 50% or more (league about 41%). Quick to abandon: 30% or less. The tooltip shows the team's run share trailing by 8+, within 7, and leading by 8+. Highlighted when it argues against our side: stubborn runners on unders, quick-to-abandon teams on overs. Tracked, not in the number or the tier (DL-Y-024).
