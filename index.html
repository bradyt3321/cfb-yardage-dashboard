Gap
Our number minus FanDuel's line. Negative: FanDuel is higher, so our side is the under. Positive: the over.
Ours
The middle outcome from the matchup: expected carries times expected yards per carry, adjusted for opponents faced, then corrected for spread and total. Sacks count against rushing.
Raw · Raw + spread
Raw is the team's rushing yards per game averaged with what the opponent allows. Raw + spread corrects that for the spread and total.
Game script
Read from the spread, same rule as the matchup dashboard. A favorite runs more late, which pushes rushing up; an underdog abandons the run, which pushes it down. Under 7 points is a close game with no lean; 14 or more shows a double arrow. "With us" means the script pushes the same way as our side of the line. "Rain leans run" shows at a 50%+ rain chance. The spread is already inside our number, so this is context for the bet and not extra yards.
Middle half
The band where the result lands half the time. A line inside it is within one game's normal swing of our number.
Book beyond all 3
FanDuel's line is 20 or more yards above, or 20 or more below, every one of our three numbers: ours, raw, and raw + spread.
Weather
Wind in mph, gusts where the site lists them, and chance of rain. Highlighted at 15+ mph wind, 25+ mph gusts or 50%+ rain. Not in our number.
Mobile QB · QB change
Tracked, not in the number (DL-Y-022). The expected starter is the QB who started the team's most recent FBS game (most dropbacks). MOBILE QB: he averages 35+ rushing yards or 6+ carries per FBS start this season, counting designed runs and scrambles only (no sacks, no kneel-downs), with at least 2 starts; with fewer starts the row shows "QB: 1 start" instead. QB CHANGE: the expected starter is not the QB with the most starts this season. "QB unconfirmed" links a dated report that the starter is questionable or not named. Highlighted on under rows, where a running QB adds rushing yards the box score counts.
Run-first
The team ran on 65% or more of its plays this season (rush attempts including sacks, divided by rush plus pass attempts, FBS games before this week), or it is an option team: Army, Navy, Air Force or Rice. Stubborn runner · Quick to abandon

Stubborn runner: 50% or more (league about 41%). Quick to abandon: 30% or less. The tooltip shows the team's run share trailing by 8+, within 7, and leading by 8+. Highlighted when it argues against our side: stubborn runners on unders, quick-to-abandon teams on overs. Tracked, not in the number (DL-Y-024).
